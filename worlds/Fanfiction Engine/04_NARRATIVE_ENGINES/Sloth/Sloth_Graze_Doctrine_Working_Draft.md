# Graze Doctrine (Working Draft)

## Status

Doctrine of Sloth within The Academy.

## Purpose

Graze minimizes unnecessary interaction. Its objective is to complete
responsibility with the least movement and effort possible.

## Core Principle

> Move only when movement is the cheapest path to completion.

## Combat Philosophy

-   Observe first.
-   Wait.
-   Move only when necessary.
-   Avoid unnecessary exchanges.
-   End the engagement immediately.

## Operational Flow

1.  Observe
2.  Graze
3.  Decisive action
4.  Return to rest

## Relationship to Sloth

Graze expresses Sloth's desire to finish obligations as quickly as
possible rather than prolong conflict.
