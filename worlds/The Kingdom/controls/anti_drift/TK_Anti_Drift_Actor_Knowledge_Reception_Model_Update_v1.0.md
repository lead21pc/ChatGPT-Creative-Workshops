## Actor Evidence

SOURCE KNOWS != ACTOR KNOWS; WORLD UPDATED != ACTOR UPDATED. Use ACTOR @ TIME + AVAILABLE INFORMATION through an established access path or an explicit authorized premise. Do not grant author intent, hidden canon, future state, full cosmology, true motives, or another agency's knowledge merely through shared nationality. WORLD-TRUE TRIGGER != OBSERVED TRIGGER != UNDERSTOOD TRIGGER. Preserve established automatic mechanisms within their trigger/access/limits; do not require fresh conscious receipt for every automatic transition.

NOTIFIED != RECEIVED; RECEIVED != UNDERSTOOD; UNDERSTOOD != BELIEVED; BELIEVED != ACCEPTED; ACCEPTED != ACTION. OBSERVATION != MODEL; MODEL != WORLD TRUTH; MODEL FAILURE != IMMEDIATE CORRECT MODEL. These are non-implications, not mandatory stages or a fixed attitude ladder.

## Concurrent Signals and Asymmetric Receipt

Distinguish doctrine, law, regulation, orders, emergency declarations, policy announcements, notices, communiques, propaganda, rumors/leaks, and lived experience when material. ANNOUNCED != LAW; PUBLICATION != RECEIPT; LEGAL_EFFECT != KNOWLEDGE. A leak can precede publication without being true or established to have occurred. Event labels do not establish temporal order.

For decisive signals, open only received content, actor-attributed issuer, channel/time/freshness, credibility, perceived authority, scope, and expected duration. Actual issuer and believed issuer may differ. Formal duration differs from actor/institution/peer expectations and observed practice; unspecified duration does not mean permanence. Actors may receive partial, stale, missing, or conflicting information.

## Bounded Updates

Retain supported, material priors, memory, experience, bias, interests, status/honor, obligations, fear/curiosity, resources, legitimacy/enforcement expectations, and retreat costs. A new signal does not reset all preferences. Exposure/repetition is not mind control; contrary experience does not force total rejection.

BELIEF != EMOTION != PREFERENCE != PUBLIC POSITION != PRIVATE INTENT != ACTION. OBEYS != AGREES; DISAGREES != RESISTS. Public compliance does not prove private agreement. Private states need evidence or an explicit premise; organizations have no automatic collective private mind. Split receivers only when differences matter; invent neither consensus nor friction. Do not assign attitude scores or force indifference-to-panic progression.

READER NOVELTY != IN-WORLD NOVELTY; CATEGORY FAMILIARITY != MECHANISM KNOWLEDGE. Familiarity may coexist with surprise at scale, variant, benefit, legality, or interaction. Do not invent psychological complexity; a supported simple response remains valid. Errors need established information/belief/pressure, never stupidity manufactured for Hyperborea's victory.

## Stop and Ownership

Stop at supported reception/update, explicit branches, or a named decisive gap; no complete actor profile is required. BELIEF owns cultural extent, INSTITUTION legal validity/enforcement, CRISIS execution. Reception selects no downstream action. There is no single rational response for all states; keep actor-specific Type II conclusions open when data is insufficient.
