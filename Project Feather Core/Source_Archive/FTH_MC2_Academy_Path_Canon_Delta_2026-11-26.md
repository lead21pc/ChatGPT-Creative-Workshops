# Project Feather Core — MC2 Academy Path Canon Delta

> **Date:** 2026-11-26  
> **Type:** canon delta / Canon 2 pathway decision  
> **Scope:** MC2, Battlemage Academy, Canon 2, post-Clash #1 multi-year interval, pre-Clash #2 causal structure  
> **Status:** USER-CONFIRMED CANON DELTA  
> **Boundary:** This delta fixes the Academy as MC2's main Canon 2 pathway for the multi-year interval after Clash #1. It does not yet fix the admission mechanism, institutional protection mechanism, exact duration, exact pre-Clash #2 crisis, or technical Clash #2 mechanics.

---

## 1. Core decision

In Canon 2, after the trajectory diverges at Clash #1, MC2's main development path will proceed through the Battlemage Academy at the northeastern Tiring Slope site.

```text
V0.5
→ Clash #1
→ Canon 2 divergence
→ MC2 enters the Battlemage Academy pathway
```

This replaces Undie as MC2's main pathway for the multi-year interval leading toward Clash #2.

Undie remains part of current canon as a professional ecosystem and may still exist elsewhere in the world or intersect other actors. This decision does not remove Undie from Project Feather Core.

---

## 2. Function of the Academy pathway

The Academy pathway provides a multi-year institutional interval in which MC2 is not continuously positioned at the center of AetherFire court politics.

Its story/worldbuilding function is:

```text
MC2 enters Academy pathway
→ MC2 develops inside a bounded institutional environment
→ AetherFire and other world systems continue accumulating live history
→ Section 1 can develop with reduced direct dependence on MC2
→ Section 2 continues through MC2's own Academy trajectory
```

This does not mean MC2 ceases to have political value.

MC2 remains:

```text
royal claimant
+ True Crown legitimacy node
+ Raging Fire / Raging Phoenix bearer
+ strategic dynastic variable
```

Her removal from immediate court politics does not erase the political interests surrounding her.

---

## 3. Relationship to the Army and True Crown

Current canon already establishes that the Army supports the True Crown.

This creates a compatible basis for the Academy pathway, but this delta does **not** automatically establish that:

```text
Army supports True Crown
→ Army has unilateral custody over MC2
→ Academy automatically protects MC2
→ nobles are legally unable to interfere
```

The exact authority/interface linking Army support, Academy jurisdiction, and MC2's protection remains unresolved.

The intended design direction is that the Academy can become a materially distinct institutional environment from direct noble control, rather than a magical immunity from politics.

---

## 4. Multi-year interval

The Battlemage Academy currently uses a six-year training model.

This makes it structurally possible for MC2 to remain on the Academy path for several years while Canon 2 accumulates independent live history.

The exact mapping between MC2's presence and the six-year training structure is not yet fixed.

Do not infer yet:

- exact entry year;
- exact age at entry;
- whether MC2 starts from Year 1;
- whether prior competence changes placement;
- whether she completes all six years;
- whether Clash #2 occurs before or after graduation;
- exact number of years MC2 remains physically at the Academy.

The currently accepted function is only:

> MC2 remains on the Academy pathway for a substantial multi-year interval before Clash #2.

---

## 5. Section 1 / Section 2 separation during this interval

During this interval, Section 1 and Section 2 may develop in parallel without requiring MC2 to drive every world-state transition.

```text
SECTION 1
AetherFire politics
institutions
military
trade/economy
foreign relations
other subsystems
──────────────────────────────► live history

SECTION 2
MC2
→ Academy training
→ relationships / decisions / development
──────────────────────────────► actor history
```

The two views remain part of the same world.

The Academy interval is therefore not a separate world or detached side story. It is a bounded actor trajectory occurring inside the same Canon 2 world-state.

---

## 6. Canon 1 remains unchanged

This decision does not alter Canon 1.

Canon 1 retains the established trajectory:

```text
MC2 concedes
→ nobles push/elevate her onto the throne
→ MC2 becomes Queen
→ MC2 dies
→ martyr / dynastic-political consequences
→ favorable conditions for MC2.2 to legitimize invasion
```

The Academy pathway belongs to Canon 2 after Clash #1 divergence.

---

## 7. V0.5 death condition remains active

This decision does not erase or rewrite the V0.5 death condition.

```text
V0.5 already contains MC2's original fatal trajectory
→ Clash #1 alters the path
→ MC2 enters Academy pathway
→ original death condition remains deferred
```

Therefore:

```text
ACADEMY PATH
!=
CANCELLATION OF V0.5 DEATH CONDITION
```

The Academy provides a different causal environment, not immunity from the underlying unresolved condition.

---

## 8. Relationship to Clash #2

Clash #2 keeps its currently established causal function:

```text
Canon 1:
V0.5 death condition already fulfilled

Canon 2:
same V0.5 death condition still deferred

→ causal incompatibility
→ Clash #2
→ MC1 enters Canon 2
```

This delta does **not** yet determine what exact event ends or breaks the Academy interval and places MC2 back into mortal danger before Clash #2.

That transition remains open.

---

## 9. Superseded pathway reading

The previous current design direction:

```text
MC2
→ Undie + identity/appearance transformation
→ covert access toward mother / Raging Fire / AF politics
```

is superseded **as MC2's main Canon 2 pathway for the multi-year pre-Clash #2 interval**.

This does not mean every Undie-related capability, institution, or possible future interaction involving MC2 is deleted.

Only its role as the primary backbone of MC2's current Canon 2 development is replaced by the Academy pathway.
