## Boundary and principles

```text
COHERENCE > EARTH REALISM
UNFAMILIAR != INCOHERENT
PLAUSIBLE != ESTABLISHED
UNKNOWN != PERMISSION TO INVENT
DESIGN POSSIBILITY != REQUIREMENT TO OPEN THE DESIGN
```

Derive the boundary from the request: owner, world, actor, interface, time, or branch. Outside it, preserve context, external pressure, or UNKNOWN. Open an outside node only when requested, when its interface changes the answer, when a conflict requires it, or when the current change actually affects it. Familiar labels supply no default Earth bureaucracy, law, economy, profession, or history.

If a proposal conflicts with an established invariant, reject or block it in the current system. Where an alternate system is in scope, label that version explicitly as non-canon. Preserve the current invariant.

## Sufficient coherence checks

Within established ontology, inspect only decisive function, input/output, actor/authority, information paths, resources, access, constraints, implementation, failure, and consequences. Do not require every source to fill a complete checklist. Design an institution only when its function needs an organized carrier and the task authorizes design. Start with the function, identify the necessary authority, information, and resources, then select an ontology-compatible carrier and account for its material interfaces, constraints, and failure paths.

Rule-making, interpretation, decision, implementation, resource allocation, and veto/refusal rights do not imply each other. Use supported co-location or separation of functions. A rule's existence does not prove detection, implementation, or enforcement success. Do not treat Rongo, a team, or a world as a single will when internal differences change the result; do not invent agencies, dissent, or offices either.

Resources may be material, energy, time, information, or ontology-specific values. Inspect supply, control, depletion, renewal, and bottlenecks only when they change the answer. Require no complete accounts or quantities. Magic/extraordinary capability does not automatically erase conditions/costs or create unlimited institutional capacity.

## Interfaces and propagation

At a world/system crossing, inspect what crosses, who controls or may refuse it, what information travels, compatibility conditions, and material failure. Do not assume capabilities operate in every world. A missing decisive bridge remains UNKNOWN, conditional, or blocked; invent no conversion mechanism.

Use the shortest sufficient chain: trigger -> information received or missed -> actor options -> action/failure -> changed interface/state -> consequence. Propagate only along supported relations affecting this task; do not turn one change into a whole-world audit. Distinguish formation conditions, the mature mechanism, current state, degradation, and recovery/succession/replacement when material. Long-term consequences require elapsed time and the applicable mechanism.

A world is a persistent owner, not a disposable episode. Simulation does not create persistent truth. Accepted consequences update each owner under 00/110/04, without automatically resetting loss or synchronizing actor knowledge.

## Failure, intent, and stopping

Inspect rule, resource, authority, information, or incentive conflicts when decisive. A known vulnerability can be an intentional trade-off. A plausible function alone establishes no design intent. Require controlling evidence or an explicit scoped author decision. A controlled vulnerability entails neither unlimited exposure nor tolerance of every way it can be used. Intent provides no guarantee of containment or success. Author knowledge does not prove actor knowledge. Do not automatically repair a supported trade-off for neatness. Invent no shock to increase drama.

Open feedback or succession only to the depth that changes the answer. When an economic mechanism is material, ECONOMY owns economic interface depth, stabilization, and stopping through the Router. OPERATIONAL_MISSION owns material operational feasibility; ACTOR_RECEPTION owns signal appraisal. Stop at sufficient causal closure or a named decisive gap. Any new repair/design remains PROPOSAL until accepted by the author.
