# C2 — Actor Reception v1.0

> Status: ACTIVE
> Family: ACTOR_RECEPTION

## Activation and ownership

The Router FULL_FILE-loads this overlay after source loading and reconciliation when a conclusion depends on an actor receiving, understanding, trusting, appraising, or updating a signal, order, briefing, or information. MCA is required for general knowledge/access/agency boundaries. WIL is conditional on a material institutional interface; OPERATIONAL_MISSION on a material operational interface. An actor's presence does not load every family.

Inherit the C2 CI/Router and loaded state. Do not select source authority, change reconciliation, invent actor psychology, or own all actor knowledge. Source truth does not make information accessible to an actor.

## Reception is not truth or action

```text
SOURCE KNOWS != ACTOR KNOWS
WORLD UPDATED != ACTOR UPDATED
NOTIFIED != RECEIVED
RECEIVED != UNDERSTOOD
UNDERSTOOD != BELIEVED
BELIEVED != ACCEPTED
ACCEPTED != COMPLIED
COMPLIED != SUCCESSFULLY EXECUTED
```

Use only established information paths, receivers, and timing, or authorized hypothetical premises. A group briefing does not prove identical receipt/understanding by each member. A signal's existence does not prove its contents true; true information does not prove the organization knows it. Distinguish actual issuer, actor-attributed issuer, trust, and authority when material.

## Concurrent dimensions and signals

Keep belief, emotion, preference, public position, private intent, and action distinct when material. Agreement does not ensure compliance; compliance does not prove agreement. Outward conduct alone does not establish private belief. Invent neither consensus/shared memory nor elaborate disagreement to make an actor complex.

Orders, briefings, experience, rumors, leaks, propaganda, and teammate information may operate concurrently. One new signal does not reset all priors. Preserve sourced stale, missing, delayed, or conflicting information. Missing official notice does not exclude every other information path. Invent no rumor/leak or its truth.

## Time, belief, and adaptation

Do not infer publication/receipt/verification/action chronology from event labels. Declared duration differs from expected duration. Familiarity with a capability category does not prove knowledge of its specific implementation. Exposure/repetition is not mind control; preserve any source-established supernatural effects within their exact limits.

Updates may depend on established priors, sources, incentives, constraints, and elapsed time. Preserve UNKNOWN or explicit branches when premises are missing. Require no probabilities, attitude scores, complete profiles, or mandatory reaction ladder.

## Stopping

Open only variables that can change the current appraisal or branch. This overlay chooses no action and guarantees no feasibility/success; leave those questions with their owners through the Router. Stop at a supported, conditional, or correctly blocked reception conclusion. Simulations and fixtures never automatically become canon.
