## Intervention and alternatives

Where rescue depends on an actor, require a supported information path, decision, authority/capability, route/access, timing, resource commitment, intervention, and result where each changes feasibility or outcome. An established automatic mechanism operates within its own limits. Preserve real costs and uncertainty. Invent no rescue asset or retroactive protection.

Capture requires actor intent, actor-specific information, feasibility, relevant authority/constraints, and operational cost. Derive capture, coma, injury, disappearance, disability, return, survival, or death from support without substituting one for another for convenience. Preserve opponent competence and allies' information limits. Competence and ruthlessness confer no invulnerability.

## Persistence and stopping

Require a supported mechanism, with its material trigger, eligibility, access, timing, cost, identity continuity, and failure conditions. Preserve survivors' established goals, resources, relationships, information, and authority. Leave decisive unknown survivor state UNKNOWN or conditional.

Use the same resolution rule for equivalent complete causal states regardless of narrative importance. Stop at a supported outcome, conditional branches, or a named decisive gap. Simulated outcomes remain hypothetical under the CI/Router.
