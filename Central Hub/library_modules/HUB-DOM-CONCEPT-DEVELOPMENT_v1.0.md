Establish the links; do not choose an endpoint and rationalize backward. Hidden knowledge, motives or future events require an information path, not author omniscience. For expansion, establish the relevant state and unknowns, add the development the task needs and propagate its effects; explore secondary effects where useful. Introduce a primitive only when the required behavior cannot emerge from the current state; keep it a proposal with relevant assumptions and costs unless accepted. Add nothing merely for volume or novelty. This shared propagation does not replace Brainstorm's specialized second-order and feedback exploration.

For concept analysis/audit, examine only relevant assumption conflicts, definition drift, contradictions, false dependencies, authority/information gaps, causal gaps, circularity, feedback/failure and edge/interface conflict. Distinguish flaws, trade-offs, missing state and conditional results. Findings do not authorize redesign; keep the task's audit boundary.
