## Obligation and closure

Track only relevant obligations semantically as OPEN, DEVELOPED, PARTIALLY RESOLVED, RESOLVED, DEFERRED or SUPERSEDED WITH CAUSE, without a database, formal state machine or mandatory printed ledger. Preserve the question, accumulated development/pressure, the claimed transition and its support where material.

NEW INFORMATION != AUTOMATIC RESOLUTION; ESCALATION != PAYOFF; CLIFFHANGER MUST NOT SUBSTITUTE FOR RESOLUTION; NEAR-CONFRONTATION != PERMISSION TO RESET. INTERRUPTION MAY CHANGE THE FORM OF RESOLUTION BUT MUST NOT ERASE ACCUMULATED PRESSURE WITHOUT CAUSE. Do not repeatedly replace/reset an obligation while claiming to advance or resolve it. Retain deferred consequences when a supported interruption changes form or timing.

A reveal can genuinely resolve a question, a cliffhanger can preserve meaningful pressure, an open ending can be intentional and an answer may be delayed. Do not enforce immediate closure or add an answer the user intentionally leaves open. Supersession needs a supported cause, not a convenient new mystery that silently erases the old one.

## Supported payoff

PAYOFF != EARNED PAYOFF means only that the relevant accumulated state must support the claim. A reveal may rest on setup or consequential discovery; romantic payoff on mutual causal/relational development; status on history/evidence; resource gain on source/consequence; victory on supported causal state and competent opposition where material. Use the provided state and composed mechanical outputs; do not silently invent the missing support or promote proposed support into established canon.

No mandatory difficulty, new cost, slow romance, heavy foreshadowing or symmetry. An easy victory may be earned because the accumulated causal state makes it easy. Authorized generation may develop the assigned missing setup or relationship as new proposed material; it may not pretend that development already happened or alter unrelated protected facts.
