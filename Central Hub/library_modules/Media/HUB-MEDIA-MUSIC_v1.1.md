# HUB-MEDIA-MUSIC

Module ID: HUB-MEDIA-MUSIC
Package ID: HUB-PKG-MEDIA
Runtime role: DOMAIN_PROCEDURE
Scope: Music and recording identification from uncertain or fragmented memory, analysis, comparison, recommendation and discovery.
Status: ACTIVE
Version: 1.1

Identify the relevant work, recording or performance when distinctions affect the result. Explain audible or supplied features such as rhythm, texture, arrangement, production and performance only as supported. Separate a listening interpretation from claims about personnel, intent, reception or influence.

Compare or recommend against current user criteria such as sound, mood or listening context. Describe the basis of fit and meaningful differences. For discovery, use supplied clues without asserting an unresolved track identity.

For identification, preserve each clue and its stated certainty. Distinguish audible features, remembered words or sounds, visual details, how and when the music was encountered, and clues to the version. Treat approximate lyrics and phonetic fragments as approximate. A literal mismatch alone does not rule out a candidate when the wording is uncertain.

Assess song, recording, performer and version separately. A supported song identity can leave the recording, performer and version open. Keep audio and visual clues separate unless evidence links them to the same recording or version. Treat listening dates, stations, channels, games, films, ads, discs, devices and platforms as access context. Release year and artist origin require separate support. A voice impression is an audible clue and does not by itself establish performer identity.

Keep plausible alternatives and conflicting clues visible, and allow fragments to come from different versions or sources. When clarification would help, ask a neutral question that distinguishes the possibilities without suggesting a candidate or answer. When outside evidence would help, state the specific question for Hub. Weigh returned findings against matches and contradictions and report which identities are supported and which remain open.

Return material external questions to Hub with the relevant release, performance or availability scope; use scoped findings and retain gaps.
