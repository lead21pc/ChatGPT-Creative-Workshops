## Strategies

### P1 — FIT

Prioritize the assigned naming system, material language/culture/region/time and structural constraints, and current concept/protected state. Add no rarity, novelty, symbolism or character-reflective-meaning objective unless the instruction assigns it.

### P2 — DISTINCT

Keep FIT and add avoidance of overly familiar/model-default or cliché choices within the assigned system. The explicit P2 selection supplies this distinctness objective; ordinary instructions may further bound it. Do not maximize rarity or obtain novelty through random phonology, pseudo-language or pseudo-fantasy strings masquerading as the assigned system. An explicitly invented naming system remains valid; do not impose an Earth-language requirement on it.

COMMON != WRONG. UNCOMMON != BETTER.
NOVELTY != RANDOMNESS. ANTI-CLICHÉ != EXOTICIZATION.
MODEL-PRIOR FAMILIARITY != REAL-WORLD FREQUENCY.

Do not assert that a name is rare, common or cliché in a real community without supporting evidence. Model familiarity is not frequency data. If such a factual distinction determines the result, return the evidence need to Hub; otherwise keep the preference bounded without pretending it is measured.

### P3 — SEMANTIC

Keep FIT and accurately handle the material semantic layer: written form, reading, segmentation/semantic units, etymology, historical usage, title/function, association, allusion or an explicitly intended fiction relation. Analysis of meaning need not make meaning a selection criterion. P3 does not require symbolism, a character-reflective name or a fiction role inferred from cultural origin. Natural-language instruction can combine distinctness and semantic work; add no P4 or preset-composition syntax.

## Naming-local distinctions in every strategy

Apply these whenever material, including plain Naming, P1 and P2; they do not depend on P3 or Anti-Prior selection:

ROMANIZATION != UNIQUE WRITTEN FORM.
CHARACTER != SEMANTIC UNIT.
LITERAL GLOSS != NAME FUNCTION.
ETYMOLOGY != CURRENT CULTURAL FUNCTION.
CULTURAL ASSOCIATION != FICTION ROLE.
NAME MEANING != CHARACTER MEANING.
MEANING AVAILABLE != MEANING REQUIRED.
SOURCE MEANING != AUTHORIZED SYMBOLISM.

Do not infer a unique written form or meaning from ambiguous romanization. Establish actual semantic units rather than automatically glossing each character. Keep written form, reading, literal sense, etymology, current name/title function and cultural association distinct. Unsupported semantic relations are not used as established support; keep dependent relations UNKNOWN or omit them without blocking sufficient independent work. A labeled fictional invention must not masquerade as an attested linguistic fact.

A necessary earlier question to establish an evidence target is ordinary clarification, not an additional gate.
